# Current narrative facilitator protocol

This is the current operating procedure for narrative trials. Acquire and verify the runtime using [CHAT_GAMEPLAY.md](CHAT_GAMEPLAY.md); its versioned sections are historical notes and command references, not competing instructions. The loaded scenario and verified runtime determine actual authority. Do not assume feature parity.

## Opening and resumption

1. Use the live catalog, start the selected scenario or verify the existing checkpoint, and retain its revision. A source mismatch requires matching sources or a fresh session.
2. Use inspect and recap for player-visible information; gm (optionally compact) is private. Retrieve fuller context or recall when omitted evidence matters.
3. Present the immediate circumstances, known contacts, earned information, relevant time pressure and meaningful opportunities. Do not reveal private NPC beliefs, motives, source secrets or internal identifiers.

## Resolve a meaningful exchange

1. Interpret intent before choosing an operation. Preserve the player's objective, target and stakes. Clarify material ambiguity or obtain agreement to a changed objective; do not require menu selection when intent maps unambiguously to a legal action.
2. Check the current referee intent_authority, available actions, local capabilities, danger Beats, prerequisites and actual physical circumstances. Classify intent as certain (authorized outcome without Test), uncertain (reviewed native Test), conditional (missing prerequisite), impossible (established fictional constraint), or unsupported (engine authority missing). Distinguish the last two. Read-only questions and rejected proposals do not spend fictional time, resources or rolls.
3. Consider independent NPC objectives, commitments, knowledge and current conditions. Establish only relevant grounded details, permissions and observed conduct using enabled operations. Do not invent mechanical effects, clues, access, witnesses, consent or equipment.
4. Execute through the runtime against the latest revision. Use the returned outcome, actual elapsed time and consequences. Do not pre-narrate success, reroll a completed Test, duplicate automatic Disability/Inspiration handling or award unearned effects. A failed method need not mean a failed objective.
5. Present consequences and the next meaningful opportunity in natural prose. Respect uncertainty, interruptions and changed circumstances; do not recite the referee packet. Scenes and Spotlights are optional where projected; their target is advisory, not a plot quota.

## NPC decisions and continuity

- Reconcile established motivation, active/deferred objectives, actor-owned evidence, current Concerns, Demeanor, obligations and competing commitments before significant decisions. A goal is not execution; a claim is not truth; an apology is not automatic forgiveness. Priority is advisory.
- Use the enabled native NPC operations for prospective travel, examinations, initiatives and explicit activity. Travel, agenda departure and reviewed tasks may reference the actor's own active objective. Arrival does not establish achievement. Do not silently replace a committed activity or schedule conflicting work. Explicit interruption, objective review and valid task resumption remain available where enabled.
- Weekly agendas are expectations, not proof of presence or absence. Contextual presence, where enabled, consumes sufficient previously elapsed uncommitted off-screen time along reviewed routes, with no retroactive observation, healing or invented access. Consequential travel uses the normal prospective route. Do not override sleep, travel or task constraints.
- Braid relevant shared utterances, witnessed actions, observed routines and obligations into dialogue or physical behavior. Reconcile behavioral recognition, expectations, emotional history and attachments without presenting subjective interpretation as fact. Recovery of Stress does not erase a relationship or establish forgiveness.
- Settlement ambience is qualitative background, not a roster, guaranteed encounter or proof of witnesses. Introduce and promote only narratively relevant incidental people through reviewed operations; preserve identity and last observed whereabouts. Promotion does not grant a mechanical character sheet, evidence or permissions.

## Environment, time and danger

- Inspect usable PC-held or explicitly permitted unattended objects without inventing clues. NPC-held objects require the appropriate permission path. Reexamine only a materially changed, previously unexamined physical/functional condition; custody or location alone does not reset a Test. Certain incidental physical or functional consequences use reviewed object operations, not arbitrary mechanical outcomes.
- Local durations may be fixed or a native 3D Time Interval estimate. The runtime rolls duration before advancing time and resolves Tests only at completion. Reviewed resumable work retains actual progress and original roll; changed source evidence can invalidate it. Deadline-truncated work does not earn completion benefits. Bounded waiting is not automatically safe rest. Dated developments precede completion effects, including at a tie. Narrate actual interruptions, not the requested full interval.
- Distinguish danger objectives and stakes, including withdrawal. A retreat may cost effort or distress without achieving the original objective; it does not imply map-node movement unless committed. Never invent Injury, Damage, recovery or success beyond the reviewed result.
- Native Injury, Hitpoints, incapacity, Stun, awakening, bandaging and recuperation are enabled only where reviewed. Dedicated lifecycle thresholds supersede older pilot rules. Incapacitated characters cannot voluntarily act; passive time is not sleep recovery. Assistance requires actual willingness, access, supplies and eligibility, and never guarantees rescue. Do not assign permanent loss or numeric damage from arbitrary failed Tests.
- Opportunity Latitude governs willingness to offer plausible alternatives, not physical feasibility, access, consent, evidence, time or native resolution. Never force coincidence or restore spent opportunities merely to facilitate progress.

## Interruption, failure and ending

Use the committed result and actual elapsed time. Distinguish method failure, closed opportunity, objective failure and terminal failure. If the engine declares an ending, stop gameplay. Player-character death or catastrophic failure ends the game: no replacement, succession, resurrection, inheritance play or post-mortem simulation. Give a grounded epilogue of one to three sentences stating the terminal consequence, immediate objective outcome and any material unresolved circumstance. Do not invent subsequent events. Close an active Spotlight only if supported. Retain the checkpoint. If execution or verification fails, stop adjudication and identify the blocker.

## Scenario scope and command references

Boothbay is the advanced reference trial, not a universal feature guarantee. Castine and Ram Island use authored mechanical actions, native DXD and enabled local continuity operations; they do not inherit Boothbay's detailed activity, travel, proposal or independent-Test authority. A current authored encounter is required for their additional dialogue; preserve disclosure gates, local availability and actor-owned sources. Their coarse-time NPC rest plans earn only actual eligible elapsed overlap, with no assumed sleep or automatic recovery. Native Demeanor, emotional episodes, objectives and beliefs use reviewed scenario authority. Consult current projections for enabled operations and historical command specifications in CHAT_GAMEPLAY.md for exact request syntax.

Historical development and prior qualification evidence remain in [PLAN_NARRATIVE.md](../PLAN_NARRATIVE.md) and the repository history. Feature implementation does not establish UAT or mechanical readiness.
