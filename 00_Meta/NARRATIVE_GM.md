# Current narrative facilitator protocol

Use this operational guide for Fear! trials after acquiring and verifying the actual runtime as described in CHAT_GAMEPLAY.md. Historical version notes in that file explain individual operations, not current universal capabilities. The loaded scenario and current verified engine outputs determine what is supported.

## At opening or resumption

1. Use the live catalog and start the selected trial. Keep a verified checkpoint and its revision.
2. Use inspect and recap for player-visible context; use gm privately. In authored_actions_only or authored_actions_with_continuity mode, restrict mechanical adjudication to offered authored actions. In the latter mode, use the explicitly enabled continuity operations for established local encounters only. Boothbay supports additional reviewed proposals, dialogue, establishment and NPC operations; consult their detailed historical command specifications only when enabled.
3. Present the immediate situation, elapsed time, relevant known contacts, earned information and meaningful options. Do not present source secrets, private NPC assessments or unexplained mechanical metadata.

## Each meaningful exchange

- Interpret player intent faithfully. Offer a supported method if the intended effect is unavailable; do not silently substitute a different objective. Do not require a menu selection when intent maps unambiguously to a legal action.
- Obtain confirmation for ambiguous or consequential commitments as required by the existing gameplay procedure. Bind every mutation to the latest revision.
- In advanced trials, consider relevant NPC objectives, knowledge, observed conduct, relationships, Concerns, availability and existing obligations before significant replies. Use independent actions only when grounded and authorized. A goal is not execution; a claim is not truth; an apology is not automatic forgiveness.
- Retrieve earlier shared information proactively when relevant. Braid concise reminders into dialogue and physical behavior, rather than requiring the player to remember the transcript. Use recall or the fuller private GM projection when a compact projection omits needed evidence.
- Resolve through the engine before narrating effects. Use returned time, interruption, Test outcome and actual state changes. Do not duplicate automatic Disability/Inspiration enforcement, reroll outcomes, invent consequences or force a plot.
- Use Scenes and Spotlights only where projected; a Spotlight target is advisory. Ordinary read-only queries do not advance time. In authored-only trials the displayed duration is the whole action, not a separate verified travel estimate.

## End or interruption

Present the engine-supported result and unresolved uncertainty. Close an active Spotlight only when supported. Retain the checkpoint; if execution or verification fails, stop adjudication and identify the blocker.

## Scope

Boothbay is the advanced narrative development trial. Castine and Ram Island support authored mechanical actions and native DXD resolution, plus persistent local dialogue, subjective NPC reactions, mundane details/properties, acknowledged agreements and atomic narrative batches. They also support scoped NPC objectives, subjective hypotheses and belief revision, interpersonal Stress, prospective coarse-time rest, and native Demeanor. They do not yet support Boothbay's free-form mechanical proposals, independent NPC Tests and movement, spatial routing or Scenes/Spotlights. Common presentation does not imply feature parity. Player-led literary qualification remains pending.

For Castine and Ram Island, a completed authored encounter is required before further recorded dialogue; the NPC must be local and inside an authored encounter time window. Linked reports may not bypass their original disclosure action. Unlinked prose is still GM-judged and must not disclose unearned facts. Agreement records are acknowledgments, not permission to bypass mechanical prerequisites. Mundane details may not introduce evidence, hazards, equipment or new access. Use fresh sessions after scenario revision changes.
For Castine and Ram Island, native emotional episodes now support the existing reviewed Stress awards (minor 1, significant 3, severe 6) on actual current local exchanges. Do not charge ordinary disagreement or duplicate an incident. NPC recovery is prospective: use npc_activity with npc, mode quiet/rest, safe boolean, minutes 1–480 and grounded basis after an actual authored encounter. Only time elapsed in an accepted subsequent action earns native recovery; continuous rest credits one Fatigue per completed minute, safe Stress Recovery Rate per ten minutes, and Weariness Recovery Rate per hour, with partial intervals retained within the same plan; no assumed safety, routine or automatic recovery. Direct dialogue, an authored action engaging that NPC, or new awarded NPC duress cancels the plan. Multi-day actions do not multiply the declared rest interval. No NPC sleep simulation or automatic independent Tests in this coarse-time model. Restart older Castine/Ram Island checkpoints.
Castine and Ram Island now use native Demeanor rather than the former nonnegative relationship scale. For significant actual social harm or acknowledged reparation, use the existing social_demeanor operation with npc, claims, kind and basis. Require current authored encounter, actual player conduct, prior harm and NPC acknowledgment for reparation. Apply native rolling limits, do not double-charge an authored Test or incident, and never infer forgiveness from recovery. Public contact labels represent Demeanor; private history guides portrayal. Existing PRE Tests now use applicable Demeanor. Restart older checkpoints.

Use the existing npc_objective, hypothesis and belief_revision operations during current authored encounters. Objectives are subjective intentions, not executed actions. Source references must be actor-owned; preserve authored disclosure gates. No automatic pursuit, NPC Favor or independent Tests are enabled in these two trials.
Dated events resolve before action completion effects, including equal timestamps. If eligibility changes during an action, use the committed interruption result and do not award its intended discovery or Test. A source revision mismatch requires matching scenario sources or a fresh checkpoint; do not silently migrate or replay with changed rules. Recall can retrieve historical witnessed descriptions of previously established details; these are memories, not verified current off-scene conditions or custody. Restart older checkpoints after the revised completion semantics.

Local capability offers specify either fixed seconds or a native 3D Time Interval range. Treat the range as an estimate, not a promised completion time. Commit an eligible intent through the normal proposal path; the runtime rolls the duration before advancing fictional time and resolves any required Test only at completion. Certain activities have no Test margin, Degrees or Favor cost. A duration roll is not an additional Test. Use the committed result, including interruptions, for narration. Do not treat an interrupted block as completed or invent preparation benefits. Long successive blocks and deadline-truncated work remain pending N90.

### Contextual environmental affordances

Frame exploration, investigation, intrigue, social manipulation and adventure, not a catalog of destructive verbs. Offer close inspection of usable PC-held objects when it serves player intent; NPC-held objects use the existing permission/Lock path. An inspection completion records the activity, not an invented clue. For an incidental certain physical outcome use the existing object lifecycle operation; for exceptional uncertain equipment or structural outcomes consult native material rules and reviewed scenario authority rather than inventing durability, automatic success or a generic destruction Test. Distinguish physical integrity from functionality in narration; do not assert unsupported mechanical functional state. If disruptive action is plausible, honor it and use actual witnessed-action records, actor-specific behavioral recognition, prior emotional context and native relationship mechanics for credible consequences. Do not fabricate witnesses, force a punitive reaction, or present impossible destruction as a legitimate mechanical option.

When a mundane object's function matters, establish its optional `function` as `operational`, `impaired` or `nonfunctional`, independently of physical integrity. For certain PC-held incidental consequences, `object_action` may specify paired `function_before` (null if not yet established) and `function_after` (impaired/nonfunctional); the physical outcome can remain unchanged. Never use this to restore equipment, bypass a Test, or disable authored scenario resources. Do not invent functional ratings for every object. Observations distinguish contemporaneous witnessed behavior from later discovery; use actor-specific knowledge and plausible consequences rather than automatic penalties. An impaired or nonfunctional object remains examinable if not destroyed.

NPCs may pursue their own active objectives through existing reviewed activities: add an optional `objective` ID when scheduling `npc_native_test` or `npc_initiative`. Reconcile current motivations, duties, observed conduct, expectations, emotional continuity and actual available means before choosing. Objective review can cite own `pattern`, assessed `expectation` and actual `activity` outcomes, as well as existing claims/observations/Tests. Deferral suspends resumable examination work and cancels nonresumable linked initiative; achieved/abandoned objectives close outstanding work. Reactivate and explicitly resume a valid interrupted task when circumstances permit; do not restart a Test to fish for a better result. The GM decides whether pursuing an objective remains plausible; priority is advisory, not an automatic selection algorithm. Achievement is subjective assessment, not established world truth. Keep private intentions out of player narration except where actually expressed or observable.

Before consequential NPC dialogue or decisions, consult active objectives and their decision_evidence, recent_episodes and salient_history, and contextual_reminders (including shared_actions). Braid relevant older shared facts into natural speech or physical behavior when needed to orient the player. A historical episode does not imply current Stress, continuing anger or automatic penalties. A discovered damaged object does not prove player responsibility. Treat cited activity status as current, not a historical snapshot; historical_reference means the source was valid when cited but is no longer available in the same state. Do not narrate private motives or source records as player knowledge. Select material context rather than reciting every reminder.

### Opportunity Latitude

At session start use `game.py start SCENARIO SESSION --seed N --opportunity-latitude permissive|balanced|strict` (choose one value; default balanced unless reviewed scenario specifies otherwise). The persisted player setting is fixed at initialization; consult referee.opportunity_policy when considering alternative approaches or plausible encounters. Distinguish method failure, opportunity closure, objective failure and terminal failure. Latitude is facilitation guidance, not a native modifier, permission, resource, time credit, evidence or authority to change NPC whereabouts. An unsupported alternative remains a proposal until validated; ended sessions remain ended. Do not fabricate chance encounters or erase established circumstances to suit the setting.
