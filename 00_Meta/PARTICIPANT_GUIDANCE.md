# Participant authoring guidance

Use the canonical world repository for participant identity and dated facts. Read the applicable rules and source definitions before assigning properties. Maintain stable namespaced IDs across scenarios; do not duplicate an entity for each player perspective.

Record motivations and commitments separately from capabilities and resources. Preserve native character and institutional models. Use a minimal profile for incidental characters and richer profiles for recurring participants. Do not impose universal HEXACO scores or generic normalized national behavior ratings when the native model already specifies relevant properties.

Separate exact source values, reviewed interpretations and hypothetical game parameters. Do not extrapolate later dates backward; missing facts stay unresolved. Keep private mappings and compiled private snapshots outside the public runner. Do not derive behavior from demographic identity.

Use the participant profile compiler for reviewed structured values. Freeze the resulting profile at scenario initialization; record later changes in session state. Never expose referee-only motivations or knowledge through player-facing views. Recorded decisions, not repeated LLM prompts, are required for exact replay.

See docs/participant-profiles.md for the contract and audit boundaries.
