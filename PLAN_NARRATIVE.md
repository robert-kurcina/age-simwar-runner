## GM protocol and scenario preparation

Use native Running the Scene: **Situation & Clock → Pressure → Cues & Actions → Test → Degrees → Update Fiction & Clock**. Pressure is qualitative and concrete, not a score. Roll only for uncertain consequential actions. The GM reads player involvement, manages pacing and information flow, and uses push (developments demanding attention), pull (Hooks and Cues), foreshadowing, complications and relief. No predetermined sequence or guaranteed resolution.

Use the native Scenario Outline: Hook; Stakes & Clock; Likely Scenes; Factions & Characters; Clues & Locks; Items of Note; Consequences & Unlocks. Include a GM-only premise/hidden truth and plausible resolutions, NPC motives and knowledge, and compact scene notes (location/timing, backdrop, exits, characters/sentiments, cues/clues/locks, items, pressure). Distinguish fixed facts from flexible preparation and player-visible information. Scene notes describe possibilities, not a script. Keep actual world time separate from specific Event Clocks and the out-of-fiction session budget. Do not force a crisis, combat, or success because a turn limit approaches.

Three complementary exploration surfaces: physical (Point Maps and location connections), social (Relationships), and information (Clues & Locks). A revelation may have several independent clue paths (Witness, Record, Pattern where suitable); any adequate path can advance play. Key Drops may introduce plausible information when circumstances warrant, not fabricate evidence or guarantee success. Do not require every clue to unlock an action or every obstacle to have a lock.

Use native Event Clocks for specific approaching consequences and Encounter Tension for stochastic exposure when relevant. Preserve native trigger/overflow/check semantics; do not advance both for the same threat. For off-scene actors, use the lightest sufficient resolution: presume function, record note, clock, consequential random check, or played scene. Record opposition reactions and persistent effects. Avoid background vulnerability loops or continuous NPC simulation.

## Relationships and character-driven consequences

Relationships are a core gameplay surface, not a late enhancement. Track only consequential directed connections between PCs, NPCs and, when relevant, groups. Store the established relationship type/context, Demeanor, Emotional Commitment where applicable, and compact consequential history; derive Relationship Diagrams from records, with separate GM and player-known views. Do not require a graph database.

- Demeanor uses native -6 to +6 labels and modifiers. First impressions use the native Fate Check and PRE where appropriate; preserve authored initial relationships. Do not add parallel trust/hostility/reaction scores. NPC knowledge, motivation, perceived risk and capability remain distinct from Demeanor. Avoid rigid Demeanor thresholds as universal access gates.
- Use native Interaction Tests for ordinary persuasion and the appropriate opposed procedure for pivotal interactions; reconcile Book II Social Tests and Book IV Interaction Tests before implementation rather than silently combining modifiers. Avoid rolling for ordinary dialogue, repeated persuasion farming or automatic compliance.
- Enforce native ordinary Demeanor changes and rate limits against fictional time: decreases 1–3, maximum 3/week; increases 0–3, maximum 3/month; recovery after decline maximum 1/week; additional decrease following an increase as specified. Track temporary day-scale changes separately; extraordinary betrayal or sacrifice can override limits through a recorded GM ruling. Verify edge cases and rounding from source before implementation.
- Emotional Commitment is distinct from Demeanor, player-initiated and GM-approved, with native benefits, costs, contextual persistence, frequency limits and later vulnerabilities. Do not infer a PC's feelings from their choices. Use native relationship-related Stress and applicable Tests; a Test of hesitation/composure does not veto a player's declared decision.
- Support Hooks & Cues and Inspiration Flashbacks through recorded plausible context and native limits; do not retroactively contradict established facts. Social Network Favors are distinct from Favor dice and are deferred until needed. Do not port setting-specific kinship generation, rank systems, or character-creation awards indiscriminately into Fear!.
- NPC commitments, disclosures, refusals, requests, and off-scene actions should follow established relationships and circumstances and produce observable consequences when relevant. A precaution must affect later play or resolution, not merely an ending score.

## Implementation boundaries

Keep one shared Python engine and existing JSON/CLI/ZIP/Chat execution. Reuse current state, predicates, events, DXD and resource guards first. The LLM GM owns scene framing, dialogue, pacing, qualitative assessment and proposals; Python owns validated persistent state, applicable tests, RNG, clocks, timing and replay. Consequential improvisation must be an accepted recorded input, with actor, intent, relevant facts, test/context and proposed effects validated before commitment. Resolve and commit events in stable order; rejected proposals have no state/time/RNG side effects. Exact replay concerns accepted mechanical inputs and outcomes, not regenerated prose. Do not allow the GM to change hidden truth, override rolls, leak private state, or retroactively move evidence without fictional justification. Keep player-visible choices illustrative rather than exhaustive commands; offer complete supported options on request and clarify ambiguous intent without advancing time.

Narrate concrete observations, dialogue, changed circumstances and actionable stakes; distinguish fact, inference and unresolved mystery. Keep condition deltas concise, notebook and known Relationship Diagram on request, and horizontal icon/label alignment. Routine execution attestations belong in compact status or on-request diagnostics. New scenario assumptions require review and labeling; no fabricated canonical facts. Session Zero boundaries remain authoritative.

No universal narrative DSL, separate dialogue engine, emotional engagement score, complete NPC simulation, fixed branching tree, mandatory scene sequence, or new setting corpus. Use actual gameplay to justify further mechanics.
