# Narrative Chat play qualification — Boothbay

Status: pending actual player-led trial. This is a facilitator procedure, not a completed play report.

## Preparation

Use a successful main-branch Chat runtime bundle, verify its revision and checksum, extract into a fresh directory, and run the live catalog. Start Boothbay with a recorded seed and preserve the initial checkpoint. Use ordinary Chat as the player interface; the player must make the decisions. Do not share referee-only material or suggest a predetermined path.

## Observe during ordinary play

- Free-form intent: interpret reasonable requests, explain unsupported effects without inventing outcomes, and offer meaningful alternatives.
- Character continuity: when a prior exchange becomes relevant, recall it naturally in dialogue; distinguish actual observation from testimony, and current circumstances from old impressions.
- NPC initiative: allow grounded independent actions when warranted; preserve motives, availability, competing priorities, and consequences. Do not force an encounter merely to exercise a feature.
- Consequences and recovery: check whether adversity, changed relationships, rest and later interactions remain coherent without plot armor or automatic reconciliation.
- Pacing: separate Scenes, Spotlights and elapsed fictional time; do not force a conclusion from a budget or require a player to exhaust action menus.
- Resumption: preserve a verified checkpoint, inspect it and use recap/recall after a break. Confirm the same revision before further action and restore context without exposing private information.

## Record evidence

At meaningful boundaries note the actual player intent, engine revision before/after, relevant accepted operation or rejection, player-visible result, and any observed contradiction or friction. Keep private diagnostic material separate from the player-facing report. Record the player's own assessment of agency, emotional credibility, recall, pacing and consequences; do not invent feedback or treat automated tests as literary approval.

Use pass / issue / not exercised for each observed criterion. A feature that did not occur naturally is not automatically qualified or failed. Retain the actual end state (including voluntary withdrawal or unsuccessful resolution). Report defects and targeted corrections in PLAN_NARRATIVE.md with evidence references; no need for a new logging subsystem.

## Exit gate

Mechanical execution and artifact verification are prerequisites, not experiential acceptance. The author must assess an actual player-led session. If runtime transfer is unavailable, use the documented ZIP fallback; if both fail, report the blocker and stop rather than simulating results. v71 readiness remains pending until qualification and meaningful defects are addressed.
