## Review decisions

- Retain Hexaday as historical context only; do not manufacture a playable episode or add factions without a defined decision role.
- Retain the seven existing rosters pending author review. In particular, the local Adherent network is a bounded exercise participant, not a representative of all adherents; religious delegations are not governments; RWF is a funding organization rather than a sovereign authority.
- Preserve all initial quantities and thresholds until a specific balance or historical problem is demonstrated. A reserve objective competing with service objectives is intentional exercise tension, not a hard prohibition on spending.
- Objectives are independent, ordered by authored faction priorities; do not silently convert objective counts into a weighted victory score. Current reports can be stale or unknown; final actor-visible results may therefore remain unconfirmed.
- Verify the historical references against their pinned source sections before reviewed registration. Retrospective narratives require particular care to avoid importing later outcomes into opening context.
- Ask the author to approve each episode's opening date, regional scope, roster, abstract calibration and authority limits. Source byte verification does not substitute for this review.
- Existing scripted regression routes establish mechanical possibilities, not independent LLM behavior or author acceptance.

## Delivery order

Present episodes chronologically for campaign discovery. They remain independent rehearsals unless a reviewed continuity mapping exists. No automatic carry-over of credits, projects, agreements or faction knowledge.
